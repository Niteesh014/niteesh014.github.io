Solar Flare Detection
SPACE AI
PyTorchConvNeXtSDOBenchmarkNASA SDO
89.57%ROC-AUC
90.51%Recall
  • Classified solar flares on the official SDOBenchmark test set (886 samples) from NASA satellite imagery.
  • Fused three imaging channels (171Å + 193Å + HMI magnetogram) into a single 3-channel CNN input.
  • Active-Region-based data splits prevent leakage; Youden's J threshold tuning added +2.17% F1, +5.31% recall.
  • Grad-CAM attention aligned with magnetic polarity inversion lines — consistent with heliophysics research.
